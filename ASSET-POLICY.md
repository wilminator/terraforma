# Asset and contribution policy

This is the rule for what may be added to the TerraForma RPG Engine repository (the "Engine"). The Engine is public and licensed under the GNU AGPL version 3 with an additional permission (`LICENSE`, `LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md`). Everything in this repository must be something the maintainer may lawfully distribute under those terms.

## 1. What this covers

An "asset" is any non-code work in the repository: pictures, sprite sheets, tilesets, sound, music, fonts, and the data files that carry them. The same rule applies to text and data copied from another work.

## 2. Who owns what

- Assets drawn, recorded or written by Michael Allen Wilmes for this repository are released under the Engine's own terms: AGPL-3.0 with the module permission.
- Assets from anyone else are accepted only under a license listed in section 3, and stay under their own license. Adding one to this repository does not transfer or relicense it.
- A game or add-on that is a Module (`LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md`, section 1) keeps its own content and its own licenses. This policy does not reach into it, except that an add-on's assets folder carries an `ASSETS.md` record (README, "Add-ons").

## 3. Accepted licenses

A work is accepted if it is the maintainer's own, or if it is released under one of these:

- CC0 1.0 or public-domain dedications
- CC-BY 3.0 or 4.0
- CC-BY-SA 4.0
- MIT, BSD (2- or 3-clause), ISC, Apache-2.0
- SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL)
- GPL-3.0 or AGPL-3.0 (or "or later" forms)

The list is the maintainer's working policy for compatibility with the AGPL. It is not legal advice, and a license not on it is decided by the maintainer before the work is added.

## 4. Not accepted

- Any license that is non-commercial (NC) or no-derivatives (ND).
- CC-BY-SA 3.0 and older, and any share-alike license not known to be compatible with the AGPL.
- Any work whose license, author or source is unknown, or that was taken from a site or product without a stated license.
- Anything from the private Vanguard Tavern repository, and anything from DragonStar's assets.
- Work generated by a tool or service whose terms bar this use.

## 5. Records

Every asset is recorded beside it, in the `ASSETS.md` of its folder (the example game's is `src/terraforma/example/assets/ASSETS.md`): its file names, its source (author and where it came from, or "drawn for this repository"), and its license. A work that needs attribution carries the attribution there. The record is part of the change that adds the asset: a pull request that adds an asset without its record is not merged.

## 6. Contributors

By submitting a change that adds a work, you state that:

1. you made it, or you have the right to submit it under the license recorded for it;
2. the record in `ASSETS.md` is accurate; and
3. if it is your own work, you license it under the Engine's terms (AGPL-3.0 with the module permission).

## 7. Changes and removal

If a work is found to be recorded wrongly, or the license is withdrawn or turns out not to allow this use, it is removed in the next change and the record is corrected. The maintainer decides this policy and may change it; a change is recorded by editing this file, and the date of the change is in the history.
